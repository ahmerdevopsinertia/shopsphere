Small Architecture Improvement

There's one improvement I'd like to introduce during Sprint 7.

Currently, your OrderService.create() performs:

product validation
inventory validation
total calculation
order creation

The CheckoutService needs almost the same behavior, but its input comes from the cart instead of a request DTO.

Rather than duplicating this logic, we'll extract the common workflow into a reusable method (or domain-level service) that both OrderService and CheckoutService can call.

This keeps the validation rules in one place and makes future changes much easier.
